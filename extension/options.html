Chilli: settings

The PUBLIC_URL of your Chilli server. On Save, Chrome asks for access to this one address.
CAPTURE_TOKEN from the .env file on the server, the same one as in the iPhone Shortcuts. It stays in this browser only.
When a page opens, the extension sends its address and title to your Chilli server and shows on the icon how many related saves you have. Incognito tabs are skipped.
